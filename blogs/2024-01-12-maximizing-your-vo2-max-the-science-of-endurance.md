Understanding VO2 Max and Its Role in Fitness The Basics of VO2 Max and Endurance VO2 Max , or maximal oxygen uptake, is the ultimate measure of fitness. It shows how much oxygen your body can use during intense exercise. The more oxygen you use, the more energy you can produce.
